Phone Me Install Kaise Karein?

4 aasan steps me Quizee ko apne Android device par activate karein:

1

APK Download Karein

Upar diye gaye Download APK button par tap karein aur file phone me save karein.

2

Install Allow Karein

Agar Android bole "Install unknown apps", to browser ko allow karke Install par click karein.

3

Permissions Enable Karein

App open karke Accessibility Service aur Display over other apps ko ON karein.

4

Apps Lock Karein

Jin apps par quiz lagana hai unka switch ON karein. Now enjoy productive study!

⚠️

Android 13, 14 & 15 Users Ke Liye Important Note:

Agar Accessibility toggle greyed out ya "Restricted setting / Disabled for security reason" dikhaye to chinta na karein! Ye Android ka naya sideload security feature hai.
Ise 10 second me fix karein: Phone Settings > Apps > Quizee kholein > Top-right corner me 3 vertical dots (⋮) par tap karein > "Allow restricted settings" select karein aur PIN dalein. Ab Accessibility turant ON ho jayegi!
