Quick Setup

Get started on your Android device in 4 simple steps:

1

Download APK

Tap Download APK above and save the file to your device.

2

Install

Open the downloaded APK and tap install when prompted.

3

Grant Access

Turn on Accessibility and Display Over Other Apps in settings.

4

Lock & Learn

Select which apps to lock and start answering quizzes on open.

💡

Android 13+ Note: "Restricted Setting" Grayed Out?

If Android shows restricted settings: Open Settings > Apps > Quizee, tap the three dots (⋮) in the top right, and choose "Allow restricted settings". Accessibility will now turn on normally.
